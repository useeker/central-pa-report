# STORY 2 — HACC tuition cut

**Live URL:** https://www.centralpareport.com/hacc-cuts-tuition-fall-2027  
**Visual hook:** **HACC Cuts Tuition by About $40 a Credit**

### (1) TikTok reel script (~100 sec)
Got a high school junior mapping college costs — or thinking about going back for nursing or trades without leaving Central PA? Listen up.

Central PA’s largest community college is cutting tuition.

On Tuesday, HACC’s Board of Trustees voted to lower tuition for most students starting fall 2027.

Here's the drop.

Who: HACC, Central Pennsylvania’s Community College — campuses in Gettysburg, Harrisburg, Lancaster, Lebanon, and York.

What: Sponsoring-district residents drop from one eighty-nine seventy-five to one fifty per credit. Other in-state students drop from two ninety to two fifty per credit.

When: Fall 2027.

Why now: HACC compared prices with all fifteen Pennsylvania community colleges, the state system, and regional schools — then reset. Approving early gives families about ten months to plan.

President and CEO Dan Lufkin’s goal: quality programs tied to the jobs this region needs, at a price families can afford. Nursing. Dental hygiene. Business. Technology. Trades.

So why does this matter?

Employers move where the workforce is. A cheaper path to a credential means more local talent, more jobs filled close to home, and more families who can afford to stay — which is what keeps housing demand steady around our campuses.

Strong schools keep a region growing.
